# Lucid Plans

Plan parameters and pricing for all four Lucid products, taken from the Lucid pricing page
("Customize your plan").

**Conventions used throughout**

- Prices marked **coupon** are the price paid with code `VAULT` at checkout, which is a
  flat **30% off** the list price on every plan and every configuration.
- The struck-through **list** price on the page does **not** update when configuration
  toggles are changed. It always shows the base-configuration list price. Coupon prices
  are therefore the authoritative figures.
- **Every price here was read from a plan card.** None is derived.
- "—" means the row is present on the card with no value. "not shown" means the row is
  absent from the card entirely.

### 5.4 The most permissive configuration

**EOD evaluation drawdown + EOD funded drawdown + DLL off** is the least constrained way to
buy a Daily account, and by a wide margin the most expensive:

| Size | Price | Reset | Cheapest Daily | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | \$160.30 | \$160 | \$50.40 | **3.2×** |
| 50K | \$235.20 | \$235 | \$65.80 | **3.6×** |
| 100K | \$350.00 | \$350 | \$110.60 | **3.2×** |
| 150K | \$490.00 | \$490 | \$160.30 | **3.1×** |

What it buys: unrealised swings never move the floor in **either** phase, and there is no
daily lockout in either phase. The intraday drawdown that otherwise defines the funded
Daily account is removed entirely.

**The trade-off that is easy to miss:** turning the DLL off forces On Demand Payouts off,
shown locked red on the card. On a product whose headline feature is daily payouts, the
most permissive risk configuration is also the one that removes intraday withdrawals and
leaves you on the end-of-day schedule.

A cheaper variant keeping On Demand Payouts — EOD eval, EOD funded, DLL **on** — appears
to exist but has not been captured.

## 8. Behaviour of the configuration toggles

- **Turning the Daily Loss Limit off forces On Demand Payouts off.** On every Pro, Flex
  and Daily card with DLL off, the On Demand Payouts toggle is locked to off. The choice is
  therefore between a daily loss cap with intraday payouts, or no daily loss cap with
  end-of-day payouts only.
- **Surcharges are applied to the discounted price.**
- **Surcharges are not independent on any multi-toggle product.** On both Flex and Daily,
  the price of each toggle depends on the position of the other. On a 25K Flex the 50%
  consistency option costs +\$4.90 with the DLL on and +\$9.80 with it off; on a 25K Daily
  Intraday it costs +\$4.90 with the DLL on and +\$10.50 with it off. **No configuration
  can be derived by addition** — every cell must be read from its own card.
- **The Daily DLL-off surcharge depends on the evaluation drawdown type** but not on the
  consistency setting: +\$14.70 / +\$24.50 / +\$39.90 / +\$49.70 under Intraday, rising to
  +\$20.30 / +\$30.10 / +\$40.60 / +\$50.40 under EOD.
- **The Flex DLL-off surcharge at 40% consistency is not monotonic in account size**:
  +\$14.70, +\$35.00, +\$44.80, then +\$39.20 at 150K. At 50% consistency it rises cleanly
  (+\$19.60 / +\$39.90 / +\$45.50 / +\$44.80), so the anomaly is confined to one row.
  The Pro surcharge rises cleanly across all four sizes.
- **Reset fees track the configured price**, rounded down to a round figure. They are not
  fixed per account size.

### 9.2 Funded rules cards

**The funded card does not vary across all evaluation configurations.** Only two toggles
can change it: the Daily Loss Limit, and on Daily possibly the funded drawdown type. The
consistency setting is not displayed on any funded card, and the eval drawdown type is not
either.

So the funded space to cover is smaller than the evaluation space:

| Product | Funded variants | Observed | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | 2 — DLL on / off | **2** | — |
| Flex | 2 — DLL on / off | **2** | — |
| Daily | 4 — DLL on / off × drawdown intraday / EOD | **3** | DLL on + drawdown EOD |
| Direct | 1 | **1** | — |

**What was learned from the DLL-off cards.** On Pro, Flex and Daily alike, switching the
DLL off changes exactly one row — the DLL reads NONE instead of a dollar amount. Every
other value on the card is unchanged.

**What was learned from the funded-EOD card.** The funded rules show "Drawdown Type: EOD"
rather than Intraday. Nothing else changes.

**What still cannot be checked.** No funded card displays a payout cap, so a card taken at
30%, 40% or 50% consistency is indistinguishable from the others. If the consistency tier
affects a funded payout cap, nothing in the captured material would reveal it. This has to
be confirmed from the help centre or support rather than from the plan cards.
