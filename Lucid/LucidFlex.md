LucidFlex Customization
Overview
LucidFlex gives traders the ability to customize their account at the time of purchase. Two settings can be adjusted to create the trading experience that best fits your risk preferences. The customization options are:

Daily Loss Limit: On or Off

Consistency: 40% or 50%

These settings create four unique LucidFlex account configurations.



Daily Loss Limit
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) determines whether a daily loss cap is enforced on your account. The DLL is a soft breach, so if you hit it you will simply be locked out until the next trading session.



Daily Loss Limit On
A Daily Loss Limit is enforced on both evaluation and funded accounts

Results in a lower evaluation purchase price

Your funded account gets On Demand Payouts (see below)

Daily Loss Limit Off
No Daily Loss Limit is applied during either the evaluation or funded phase

Results in a higher evaluation purchase price

Payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule

Consistency
40% (standard): your largest day must be 40% or less of your evaluation profit. Lowest price.

50% (add-on): your largest day can be up to 50%, so you can pass in as few as 2 days. Added at checkout for a small fee.

Consistency applies to the evaluation only. Funded LucidFlex accounts have no consistency rule. See LucidFlex Consistency Percentage.

On Demand Payouts
Keep the Daily Loss Limit On and your funded account can request payouts during the trading session, from profit made that session (6:00 PM to 3:45 PM ET).

After requesting an intraday payout, trading on your funded LucidFlex account is paused until the end of the trading day.

Accounts with a Hedging Violation are not eligible for intraday payouts. See Prohibited: Hedging.

With the Daily Loss Limit Off, payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule.

Available Account Configurations
LucidFlex offers four possible combinations.



Option 1: Daily Loss Limit ON + 40% Consistency
Daily Loss Limit enabled for evaluation and funded

On Demand Payouts

Cheapest version of LucidFlex

Option 2: Daily Loss Limit ON + 50% Consistency
Daily Loss Limit enabled for evaluation and funded

On Demand Payouts

Pass in as few as 2 days

Option 3: Daily Loss Limit OFF + 40% Consistency
No Daily Loss Limit

Regular end-of-day payouts

Option 4: Daily Loss Limit OFF + 50% Consistency
No Daily Loss Limit

Regular end-of-day payouts

Pass in as few as 2 days

Highest pricing version of LucidFlex

Choosing the Right Configuration
Your customization should reflect your priorities:

Choose Daily Loss Limit ON if you prefer a lower purchase price and On Demand Payouts

Choose Daily Loss Limit OFF if you want more flexibility and don't mind paying a premium for it

Choose 50% Consistency if you want to pass faster

Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidFlex account with your preferred options.

LucidFlex Consistency Percentage
How the LucidFlex consistency rule works: 40% standard, 50% with the add-on, the calculation, and what it means for passing.

O
Written by Operations Lead
Updated over 3 weeks ago
Consistency
The Consistency Percentage tracks a traders ability to earn profits over multiple sessions. This demonstrates a that a trader has and can adhere to a risk management strategy.



The Calculation
In LucidFlex evaluation accounts your Consistency Percentage must be 40% or less to be eligible to upgrade to a LucidFlex funded account. If you added the 50% consistency add-on at checkout, it must be 50% or less.

Use the following formula to determine if you are adhering to the Consistency Percentage:



Largest Single Day Profit / Account Profit = Consistency Percentage



Example
You trade your $50k LucidFlex evaluation account and your Largest Single Day Profit was $750 when you reached the $3,000 profit target. Plug the values into the above formula.



$750 / $3,000 = 25%



Congratulations, you are under the 40% requirement (and the 50% add-on). You may now upgrade to a LucidFlex funded account. If your largest day had been more than your limit (40%, or 50% with the add-on), you would keep trading until your largest day is back within it.



Cushion with the 50% Add-on
There is a small cushion built into the 50% consistency add-on so that traders that are super eager to pass in two days can do so. However, taking your time to reach your profit target is the best approach.

Circumventing the Scaling Plan
Some trader may be inclined to try and get around the scaling plan. Our back-end systems track and adjust balances for this. If we see repeated behavior attempting to get around the limits, we may review your account.



What is a Scaling Plan for a Futures Prop Firm Account?
A scaling plan in futures trading controls how many contracts you're allowed to trade at one time in your funded account. Your limit is tied directly to your simulated profit balance. The more you've earned, the more buying power you have access to. Unlike many prop firms, LucidFlex has no scaling restrictions during the evaluation phase. You have access to your full max contract size from your first trade.



Scaling Plan FAQs
Does the scaling plan apply during my evaluation?

No. There is no scaling plan in the LucidFlex evaluation phase.

When does my contract limit update?

At the end of each trading session. It does not update in real-time during the day.

What happens to my contract limit if I take a payout?

Since payouts reduce your simulated balance, your scaling tier may adjust downward to reflect the updated profit level.

Can I trade my full contract size from day one in the funded account?

Your starting size is determined by your initial profit level per the scaling table. As you build profits, your max size grows.

What if I accidentally exceed my contract limit?

Each platform/datafeed provider has guardrails in place to prevent/block this a much as possible, however users whom intentionally try to, or succeed in circumventing these limits may result in having profit wiped for the day.

End of Day Drawdown
LucidFlex evaluation and funded accounts use an End-of-Day Drawdown (EOD Drawdown) system to calculate the Max Loss Limit (MLL).

At the end of each trading session, the system calculates the account’s highest closing balance

The MLL increases as your balance grows, up to a defined point

Once your account exceeds the Initial Trail Balance, the MLL locks and no longer moves

Once you request a payout from LucidFlex, your MLL automatically adjusts to the Locked MLL Balance

Live Escrow
Escrow is the portion of your move-to-live capital not deposited on Day 1. It is released based on live performance.



Escrow Release Criteria



Before Escrow can begin releasing, you must meet two requirements:

Complete 10 profitable trading days in your live account

Earn $10,000 in live trading profits

Once both of these conditions are met, our risk team will review your performance.



Escrow Release Structure

For every $10,000 in additional live profits earned, $5,000 in Escrow will be released

Escrow is reviewed and calculated weekly, not daily

This process continues until the full Escrow allocation has been released

Important: Traders who engage in reckless or high-risk behavior ("yolo" trading) will not be eligible for Escrow release.
