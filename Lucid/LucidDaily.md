LucidDaily Customization
Overview
LucidDaily gives traders the ability to customize their account at the time of purchase. Two settings can be adjusted to create the trading experience that best fits your risk preferences. The two customization options are:

Daily Loss Limit: On or Off

Evaluation Drawdown: End-of-Day (EOD) or Intraday

Consistency: 40% or 50%

These settings create eight unique LucidDaily account configurations.



Daily Loss Limit
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) determines whether a daily loss cap is enforced on your account. The DLL is a soft breach, so if you hit it you will simply be locked out until the next trading session.



Daily Loss Limit Enabled
A Daily Loss Limit is enforced on both evaluation and funded accounts

Results in a lower evaluation purchase price

Daily Loss Limit Disabled
No Daily Loss Limit is applied during either the evaluation or funded phase

Results in a higher evaluation purchase price

Consistency
40% (standard): your largest day must be 40% or less of your evaluation profit. Lowest price.

50% (add-on): your largest day can be up to 50%, so you can pass in as few as 2 days. Added at checkout for a small fee.

On Demand Payouts
With the Daily Loss Limit On, you can request payouts from your funded LucidDaily account during the trading session as soon as your dashboard shows all payout objectives are complete.

You can submit multiple payout requests from the same account within a single session, provided all payout requirements are met for each request.

Accounts with a Hedging Violation are not eligible for intraday payouts. See Prohibited: Hedging.

With the Daily Loss Limit Off, payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule.

Evaluation Drawdown
This setting determines how the Max Loss Limit is calculated during the evaluation only.
​

Important: Regardless of your selection, all LucidDaily Funded accounts use an Intraday Drawdown.



End-of-Day (EOD) Drawdown
Drawdown is calculated using your account balance at the end of each trading session

Unrealized gains and losses do not affect the drawdown calculation

Results in a higher evaluation purchase price

Intraday Drawdown
Drawdown is monitored continuously throughout the trading session

Unrealized profits and losses are included in the calculation

Results in a lower evaluation purchase price

Available Account Configurations
LucidDaily offers four possible combinations.



Option 1: Daily Loss Limit ON + Intraday Evaluation Drawdown
Daily Loss Limit enabled

Evaluation uses Intraday Drawdown

Funded account uses Intraday Drawdown

Choice of 40% or 50% Consistency

Cheapest version of LucidDaily



Option 2: Daily Loss Limit ON + End-of-Day Evaluation Drawdown
Daily Loss Limit enabled

Evaluation uses End-of-Day Drawdown

Funded account uses Intraday Drawdown

Choice of 40% or 50% Consistency

Mid-tier pricing version of LucidDaily



Option 3: Daily Loss Limit OFF + Intraday Evaluation Drawdown
No Daily Loss Limit

Evaluation uses Intraday Drawdown

Funded account uses Intraday Drawdown

Choice of 40% or 50% Consistency

Mid-tier pricing version of LucidDaily



Option 4: Daily Loss Limit OFF + End-of-Day Evaluation Drawdown
No Daily Loss Limit

Evaluation uses End-of-Day Drawdown

Funded account uses Intraday Drawdown

Choice of 40% or 50% Consistency

Most expensive version of LucidDaily



Choosing the Right Configuration
Your LucidDaily customization should reflect your priorities:

Choose Daily Loss Limit ON if you prefer a lower purchase price and access to on-demand payouts on your funded account.

Choose Daily Loss Limit OFF if you prefer no daily loss cap and don’t mind paying a higher purchase price.

Choose Intraday Drawdown in Eval for a lower evaluation purchase price.

Choose EOD Drawdown in Eval if you prefer drawdown calculated at the end of the trading session and don’t mind paying a higher purchase price.

Choose 50% Consistency if you want to pass faster

Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidDaily account with your preferred options.

Profit Split Structure
All LucidDaily funded account payouts are split 90% to the trader and 10% to Lucid Trading.

Payout Eligibility Criteria
There are two requirements must be met in LucidDaily to qualify for a payout.



1. Profit Over Buffer Balance
To request a payout, your profits must exceed the buffer balance, which acts as a safety net to avoid hitting Max Loss Limit after a payout. You can not take a payout from the buffer balance.

The buffer is equal to: Initial Max Loss Limit + $100

Consistency
The Consistency Percentage tracks a traders ability to earn profits over multiple sessions. This demonstrates a that a trader has and can adhere to a risk management strategy.



The Calculation
In LucidDaily evaluation accounts you must have Consistency Percentage of 50% or less to be eligible to upgrade to a LucidDaily funded account.

Use the following formula to determine if you are adhering to the Consistency Percentage:



Largest Single Day Profit / Account Profit = Consistency Percentage



Example
You trade your $50k LucidDaily evaluation account and your Largest Single Day Profit was $750 when you reached the $3,000 profit target. Plug the values into the above formula.



$750 / $3,000 = 25%



Congratulations, you have maintained consistency under the LucidDaily evaluation 50% requirement. You may now upgrade to a LucidDaily funded account. However, if your largest day had exceeded 50% of your account profit, you would need to continue trading until your largest day is 50% or less.



Consistency Cushion
There is a small cushion built into the LucidDaily consistency so that traders that are super eager to pass in two days can do so. However, taking your time to reach your profit target is the best approach.

A
Written by AJ
August 19, 2026
Customization
Depending on what option you choose at checkout, you may or may not have a Daily Loss Limit for your LucidDaily accounts.

If you chose DLL ON then you will have a fixed DLL on both your LucidDaily evaluation and funded accounts

If you chose DLL OFF then you will not have a DLL on either your LucidDaily evaluation or funded accounts

So, depending on your option you either have a DLL on both phases, or none in either phase.



Daily Loss Limit
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) is the maximum amount a trader can lose in a single day before trading is restricted until the next session. All DLLs at Lucid Trading are considered soft breaches, meaning you do not lose your account for hitting DLL as long as the Max Loss Limit (MLL) has not been reached.



Please note: The DLL remains fixed throughout the trading session and does not trail upward with the Max Loss Limit (MLL). If the MLL trails above the DLL during the session, the MLL becomes the more restrictive threshold. Reaching the MLL at that point will result in a hard breach.

Being Evaluated for Live from LucidDaily
Lucid's risk team continuously monitors trader performance to determine when a trader is ready to transition to live futures trading.
​

Traders enter the live review pool when one or more of the following conditions are met:

If they earn the Maximum Daily Profit for their account size

After being paid out a significant amount of capital lifetime

If they have exceptional performance in sim funded

If they have previously been moved live

All live transitions occur at the discretion of the Lucid risk team.



Sim Profits When Moved Live from LucidDaily
Sim profits are handled differently on the transition to live from LucidDaily compared to our other plans.

Buffer Balance
The buffer balance that was built in the funded account is used to fund the starting live drawdown. This may not be withdrawn.

Sim Profits Above the Buffer
Sim profits above the buffer may be paid out to the trader once their KYC with the broker partner has been completed and the sub accounts have been approved.



The amount is capped at a fixed amount of $15,000.



The cap is the same regardless of account size or how many you have. Whether you have one 25k account or five 150k accounts, the maximum sim profit paid out on the move to live is $15,000. Any profits in excess of the capped amount may not be withdrawn.



Transition to Live
When a futures trader is moved to live from LucidDaily:

Traders receive one live account per eligible funded account

Each funded account must have received at least one payout to qualify

$0 starting balance

No daily loss limit

IMPORTANT
We move traders live every evening after the final session report comes in at 6 PM est. This process can take several hours. When a trader is moved live, their trading accounts are paused and all positions are flattened.



This mean in some cases, LucidDaily funded traders will be moved live and flattened while in an active position. In the spirit of fairness to all traders, we will adhere to the following policy.
​
​When you are moved live in a LucidDaily funded account, we will be using your prior day closing balance as the amount you are moved live with. Any profits or losses made in the session you are moved live will not be counted. This ensures every trader is treated equally.



Consistency Rules at Live
There are no consistency requirements for live accounts.



Locking the Live MLL
Lucid Live accounts use an End-of-Day Drawdown (EOD Drawdown) system to calculate the Max Loss Limit (MLL).

At the end of each trading session, the system calculates the account’s highest closing balance.

The MLL increases as the account balance grows, up to a defined point.

Once a trader generates live profits equal to their starting live drawdown, the Max Loss Limit locks at $100 and no longer moves.

If a trader requests a live payout before generating profits over the live drawdown, the Max Loss Limit locks to $100.



Cooldown
Traders who have been moved live may return to purchase a new evaluation after blowing a live account..

Standard cooldown period: 2 weeks

Traders who repeatedly blow live accounts due to reckless or "yolo" behavior may be subject to longer cooldowns at the discretion of the risk team

LucidMaxx traders:

LucidLive Account FAQ
Do all funded prop accounts move live at once?
Yes. All eligible funded prop accounts (with at least one payout) are moved when a trader transitions to live.



What happens to remaining simulated prop accounts?
All simulated prop accounts are closed when a trader is moved live. If a funded account is not moved live because it has 0 payouts, the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded. If you are not comfortable with evaluations being closed, do not keep evaluations in reserve.



If I am live, can someone in my household still trade sim?
No. If one household member is trading live, others may not trade simulated accounts.



Can I hedge my live accounts?
Absolutely not. Hedging live accounts is strictly prohibited by both Lucid and the CME. Violations will result in a permanent ban.
