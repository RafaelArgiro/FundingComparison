LucidPro Customization
Overview
LucidPro gives traders the ability to customize their account at the time of purchase. One setting can be adjusted to create the trading experience that best fits your risk preferences. The customization option is:

Daily Loss Limit: On or Off

These settings create two unique LucidPro account configurations.



Daily Loss Limit
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) determines whether a daily loss cap is enforced on your account. The DLL is a soft breach, so if you hit it you will simply be locked out until the next trading session.



Daily Loss Limit On
A Daily Loss Limit is enforced on both evaluation and funded accounts

Results in a lower evaluation purchase price

Your funded account gets On Demand Payouts (see below)

Daily Loss Limit Off
No Daily Loss Limit is applied during either the evaluation or funded phase

Results in a higher evaluation purchase price

Payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule

On Demand Payouts
Keep the Daily Loss Limit On and your funded LucidPro account can request payouts during the trading session, from profit made that session (6:00 PM to 3:45 PM ET).

After requesting an intraday payout, trading on your funded LucidPro account is paused until the end of the trading day.

With the Daily Loss Limit Off, payouts follow the regular end-of-day schedule.

Available Account Configurations
LucidPro offers two possible combinations.



Option 1: Daily Loss Limit ON
Daily Loss Limit enabled for evaluation and funded

On Demand Payouts available

Cheapest version of LucidPro

Option 2: Daily Loss Limit OFF
No Daily Loss Limit

Regular end-of-day payouts

Higher pricing version of LucidPro

Choosing the Right Configuration
Your customization should reflect your priorities:

Choose Daily Loss Limit ON if you prefer a lower purchase price and On Demand Payouts

Choose Daily Loss Limit OFF if you want more flexibility and don't mind paying a premium for it

Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidPro account with your preferred options.

LucidPro Consistency Percentage
How the LucidPro consistency percentage works: how it is calculated, when it applies, and what it means for payout eligibility on LucidPro accounts.

D
Written by Doug
August 26, 2026
Consistency
The Consistency Percentage tracks a traders ability to earn profits over multiple sessions. This demonstrates a that a trader has and can adhere to a risk management strategy.



The Calculation
In our LucidPro funded accounts you must meet a Consistency Percentage of 40% to be eligible to request a payout. The Consistency Percentage resets and must be met again after each payout.



Use the following formula to determine if you are adhering to the Consistency Percentage:



Largest Single Day Profit / Account Profit = Consistency Percentage



Example
You trade your $50k LucidPro Account and your Largest Single Day Profit was $1,000 and your Account Profit is $4,000. Plug the values into the above formula.



$1,000 / $4,000 = 25%



Congratulations, you have maintained consistency under the LucidPro 40% requirement. You may now request a payout so long as you have met the other necessary objectives. However, if your largest day had exceeded 40% of your Account Profit, you would need to continue trading until your largest day is below 40%.



Consistency update
We adjusted the LucidPro funded consistency percentage from 35% to 40%.



It applies only to accounts purchased or reset on or after 11/28/2025 at 3:00 PM EST. All account purchased or reset on or before still have 35% consistency.



Consistency at Live Account Status
Once promoted to Live account status, the consistency requirement no longer applies. The 40% (or 35%) consistency requirement applies only during the funded account stage.

Daily Loss Limit
The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) is the maximum amount a trader can lose in a single day before trading is restricted until the next session. All DLLs at Lucid Trading are considered soft breaches, meaning you do not lose your account for hitting DLL as long as the Max Loss Limit (MLL) has not been reached.

LucidScale DLL Formula:

Live Escrow
Escrow is the portion of your move-to-live capital not deposited on Day 1. It is released based on live performance.



Escrow Release Criteria



Before Escrow can begin releasing, you must meet two requirements:

Complete 10 profitable trading days in your live account

Earn $10,000 in live trading profits

Once both of these conditions are met, our risk team will review your performance.



Escrow Release Structure

For every $10,000 in additional live profits earned, $5,000 in Escrow will be released

Escrow is reviewed and calculated weekly, not daily

This process continues until the full Escrow allocation has been released

Important: Traders who engage in reckless or high-risk behavior ("yolo" trading) will not be eligible for Escrow release.
