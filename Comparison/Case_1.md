# Case 1: Learning Only

## 1. Scope and criteria

The goal is to spend about **two months** learning futures on a simulated account, then move to own capital. Passing an evaluation and taking payouts are not goals. The profile is 1–3 trades per session, 1–2 sessions per week, with 50–100 point stops on MNQ. At one MNQ, three full losses at the widest stop cost **\$600 before fees and slippage**.

Prices below are the captured coupon prices, not a guarantee of current offers: Lucid `VAULT`, Tradeify `NOV`. All are one-time purchase prices. Totals exclude data, commissions, resets and taxes. Netherlands availability and Tradovate are supported in the captured general information, although Inter Miami's specific conditions are incomplete. Check eligibility and current checkout terms before purchase.

**Screening definitions**

- **Initial room:** Pass means the initial drawdown exceeds \$600 at one MNQ, with some room for costs. It does not guarantee survival of a later session, or of larger positions. A floor that has already trailed up can leave much less room.
- **Drawdown:** Pass means EOD trailing or static for the **phase actually traded**. Intraday trailing is less suitable because unrealised peaks can raise the floor. EOD floors can still be enforced against unrealised losses.
- **Inactivity:** Pass means a documented 30-day rule rather than a weekly trading requirement. A weekly requirement is a concern, not proof the account will fail.
- **Cost:** Pass means no more than **\$100 for the 50K baseline**, excluding extras. This is a comparison threshold, not a user-set budget.
- **?** means the available information is insufficient or contradictory. A daily loss limit does not guarantee survival: the floor may be closer and losses can overshoot the limit.

**Initial room is not the same as a two-month survival probability.** In particular, a 25K account with \$1,000 initial drawdown can absorb one \$600 session from a fresh start before costs, but that session consumes 60% of the initial room. It is not automatically a failure of the stated one-session requirement; it leaves much less room for subsequent losses. If sizing changes, all figures need recalculating.

## 2. Most suitable candidates

**Lucid Flex 50K, \$80.50:** the strongest documented fit for an EOD learning evaluation at the stated price threshold. It has a \$2,000 initial drawdown and a 30-day inactivity rule. The 25K configuration is \$45.50, but its \$1,000 drawdown makes a \$600 session much more consequential.

**Lucid Daily 50K with EOD evaluation, \$85.40:** close alternative with the same initial drawdown and inactivity rule. Its funded account is intraday trailing in this configuration, but funded terms do not matter if the evaluation is used only for learning.

**Tradeify Growth 50K, \$87:** competitive price, EOD evaluation drawdown and a DLL. Its weekly inactivity condition makes it less forgiving of a skipped week. It becomes more attractive if weekly trading is reliable.

**Tradeify Inter Miami 50K:** conditional alternative if a static floor is worth the \$263 price and the missing rules are confirmed. Static drawdown lets the cushion grow with profit from the start, but the starting \$1,500 drawdown is smaller and the rules are incomplete.

Before paying, consider an ordinary broker demo: it may be free, although availability, market-data costs, duration and platform access need checking. Paying for a prop evaluation is most useful here if experiencing its restrictions is itself part of the learning goal.

## 3. Lucid products

### 3.1 Lucid Flex

- **50K:** \$80.50, 40% evaluation consistency, DLL on, \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown and \$1,200 DLL. The 50% consistency setting costs \$90.30; it matters only if passing is desired.
- **Advantages:** lowest captured price among the 50K EOD evaluation configurations shown; 30-day inactivity rule; reset listed at \$80 for the baseline configuration.
- **Limitations:** the floor can still be breached intraday by open losses. A DLL is not a substitute for checking remaining drawdown. Evaluation profit targets and funded payouts are not needed for this case.

### 3.2 Lucid Daily

- **50K:** \$85.40 for EOD evaluation, intraday funded, DLL on and 40% evaluation consistency. A cheaper \$65.80 configuration uses intraday drawdown in the evaluation as well.
- **Advantages:** EOD evaluation at a modest premium to Flex; \$2,000 initial drawdown and \$1,200 DLL; 30-day inactivity rule.
- **Limitations:** choose the evaluation drawdown carefully. The funded news restriction and funded drawdown do not apply merely because the evaluation is traded. If continuing into funding is possible, they become important. The price of the EOD-funded / DLL-on configuration was not captured.

### 3.3 Lucid Pro

- **50K:** \$120.40 with DLL on; \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown and \$1,200 DLL; reset \$120.
- **Advantages:** EOD drawdown, 30-day inactivity rule and no evaluation consistency rule.
- **Limitations:** costs \$39.90 more than Flex at 50K. The absence of evaluation consistency adds little if passing is not the objective.

### 3.4 Lucid Direct

- **50K:** \$360.50; immediate simulated funded account, \$2,000 EOD drawdown and \$1,200 DLL.
- **Advantages:** no evaluation and the same 30-day inactivity rule; an option if the intention changes to seeking payouts.
- **Limitations:** substantially more expensive than an evaluation used as a simulator. Reset availability is unresolved. Funded payout benefits are not needed for this case.

## 4. Tradeify products

### 4.1 Growth

- **50K:** \$87; \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown, \$1,250 DLL, evaluation reset \$95.
- **Advantages:** low one-time price, no evaluation consistency requirement and a defined DLL.
- **Limitations:** the documented weekly activity requirement applies. At 1–2 sessions per week a missed week needs planning; closure follows an email warning. A reset is more expensive than the captured purchase price.

### 4.2 Select: Flex and Daily

- **50K:** one \$116 evaluation with \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown. Flex or Daily is selected on funded activation, so the two paths do not change the learning evaluation.
- **Advantages:** EOD drawdown; the option to choose a funded path later.
- **Limitations:** no DLL in the 50K evaluation, weekly inactivity requirement and higher price than Growth. The evaluation consistency rule only matters if attempting to pass. Evaluation reset is \$109.

### 4.3 Inter Miami

- **50K:** \$263 with soft \$900 DLL, or \$440 with DLL removed; \$1,500 static drawdown; Tradovate only and 30 micros maximum.
- **Advantages:** static floor avoids the loss of cushion caused by a trailing floor; the soft-DLL option is cheaper.
- **Limitations:** price is high for learning alone, and starting drawdown is smaller than the 50K alternatives above. Reset price, inactivity, enforcement details and applicability of general rules are not documented. The soft DLL does not guarantee the account survives a session.

### 4.4 Lightning

- **50K:** \$344; immediately funded with \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown and \$1,250 DLL.
- **Advantages:** no evaluation; funded account available immediately.
- **Limitations:** high price for a learning-only objective, weekly inactivity requirement and no reset. Its funded payout rules offer no benefit if no payout is planned.

## 5. Decisions and sources

The remaining decisions are whether the account must survive the entire two months, whether to allow 25K despite the smaller cushion, whether weekly activity is practical, and whether a free simulator is sufficient. There is no basis yet for a definitive survival ranking or reset budget.
