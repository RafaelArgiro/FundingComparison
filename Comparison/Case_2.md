# Case 2: Grow One Account and Withdraw Profits

## 1. Scope and criteria

The objective is to receive **\$5,000**, preferably **\$10,000**, from one funded account before moving to own capital. Amounts in hand are after the provider's split but before tax and payment fees. An evaluation or a straight-to-funded purchase is acceptable. The plan is to retain profits while building a cushion, increase position size as the cushion and stop distance permit, then potentially trade smaller while satisfying payout requirements.

The trading profile does not deliberately trade news, but accidental news exposure must not end the account. Trades normally last minutes, not seconds.

This comparison uses **50K as a starting point**, not a recommendation that 50K is always best. A larger account can supply more initial drawdown and different payout caps, but also costs more and may have a larger evaluation target. Prices are captured coupon prices: Lucid `VAULT`, Tradeify `NOV`. They are one-time purchase prices, before commissions, data, resets, taxes and payment fees; current offers need verification.

**How to read the screening:**

- **Growth:** Pass means the documented funded floor is EOD or static, so retained profit can grow the cushion after the trailing floor locks, or from the start for a static floor. It does not mean unlimited size or guaranteed survival.
- **News:** Pass means no funded news restriction is documented for the product; No means an accidental US high-impact news trade can breach the account.
- **Inactivity:** Pass means the documented Lucid 30-day rule; No means the documented Tradeify weekly rule, a practical concern rather than automatic disqualification.
- **\$5K / \$10K:** Pass means the documented payout caps do not rule out the target on the simulated account, assuming all conditions are met and no earlier transition. No means a documented cap rules it out in that phase; ? means the available rules do not establish it. None is a guarantee of achievement or of avoiding a forced live move.
- **Extraction:** Pass means no 50%-of-profit formula and no payout consistency rule documented for the funded product. No indicates one or both restrictions; ? indicates missing terms. Other hurdles, including buffers, caps and qualifying days, still apply.

## 2. Most suitable candidates

**Lucid Pro 50K:** strongest documented Lucid candidate for both targets if news-breach avoidance and 30-day inactivity are important. Its 50K caps total \$12,000 gross across five requests, or **\$10,800 at 90%**, before fees and tax. That leaves only \$800 of cap headroom over the \$10,000 target, requires every payout, and is exposed to review and transition risk. The 40% consistency rule can delay payouts after a large trading day.

**Tradeify Select Daily 50K:** strongest documented extraction formula for the retain-then-withdraw strategy: daily eligibility, no funded consistency rule and up to twice new cycle profit, subject to the \$1,250 request cap and \$52,100 buffer. It requires multiple requests and positive new cycle profit. Weekly activity and possible discretionary live transition are significant risks.

**Tradeify Growth 50K:** lower purchase cost and no evaluation consistency rule, with an EOD drawdown and defined DLL. Funded 35% consistency, five qualifying days per cycle and rising payout caps can slow extraction; a discretionary live move can interrupt the plan.

**Lucid Flex 100K:** conditional alternative if the preferred \$10,000 target matters more than the lowest price. Five published \$2,500 caps total \$12,500 gross, or **\$11,250 at 90%**, versus \$9,000 at 50K. The 50% formula, five qualifying days per cycle, a \$6,000 evaluation target and uncertainty about the definition of profit remain material.

**Lucid Direct 50K:** conditional straight-to-funded alternative. It avoids the \$3,000 evaluation target and has EOD drawdown and no funded news restriction, but costs \$360.50 and imposes a 20% consistency rule and resetting profit goals. Payout count and reset availability need confirmation.

**Tradeify Inter Miami:** potentially attractive for scaling because the floor is static. It is not a defensible shortlist selection until its payout formula, caps, inactivity and other missing rules are supplied. Lucid Daily has attractive payout access but conflicts with the stated requirement that accidental news exposure must not end the account.

## 3. Shared tradeoffs

### 3.1 Growth versus extraction

EOD trailing floors do not generally leave more room simply because the account reaches a new closing high before the floor locks. Once locked, retained gains can increase the cushion. Inter Miami's static floor can allow this from the start. In all cases, position limits, daily loss limits, open-position enforcement and losses constrain scaling.

Payouts reduce the balance. Waiting can support larger positions but leaves more profit exposed to breach or transition. Trading smaller to meet qualifying days can reduce risk, but it does not guarantee a sufficiently profitable day, and consistency rules can require additional profit after a large day.

### 3.2 What the target requires

At a 90% split, receiving \$5,000 requires at least **\$5,555.56 gross**; \$10,000 requires **\$11,111.12 gross**, rounded up to cents. At an 80% share these become **\$6,250** and **\$12,500** respectively. Caps apply to gross requests in the worked comparisons below. Confirm the provider's split, rounding and the treatment of withdrawal amounts before relying on these totals.

All illustrative cap totals assume every request is approved at its maximum and no earlier live transition occurs. They do not prove that sufficient profits, qualifying days or consistency can be achieved. A 50% formula can leave profit behind even when the caps appear high enough.

## 4. Lucid products

Lucid's general inactivity rule is 30 calendar days with the documented net-P&L condition. Its funded products discussed here use a 90% trader share. The live reports state transitions are at risk-team discretion and the current live structure starts at \$0; the treatment of remaining simulated profit must be confirmed. A documented cashout alternative has a 24-hour decision window and is based on 50% of live starting balance, subject to the split; verify its value and applicability before depending on it.

### 4.1 Lucid Pro

- **Purchase and risk:** 50K DLL-on evaluation \$120.40; \$3,000 target; \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown in both phases. Funded DLL begins at \$1,200 and converts to LucidScale after the trail phase. No evaluation consistency rule and full documented contract size from the start.
- **Payout:** 50K requires a \$52,100 buffer, 3 trading days and 40% consistency. First cap is \$2,000, then \$2,500 for payouts 2–5. There is a \$500 minimum. The five caps yield at most \$10,800 after split, before fees and tax.
- **Advantages:** EOD drawdown, no documented funded news restriction, no 50% payout formula, and enough published cap capacity for both targets.
- **Issues:** an outsized day can require further profit before the 40% rule is satisfied; five payouts are needed for \$10,000 and a move can occur at the firm's discretion. The buffer and any desired trading cushion must be built as well as the withdrawal. On 100K the caps are \$2,500 then \$3,000, totalling \$14,500 gross across five, or \$13,050 after split; the evaluation target rises to \$6,000.

### 4.2 Lucid Flex

- **Purchase and risk:** 50K DLL-on / 40% evaluation \$80.50; \$3,000 target; \$2,000 EOD drawdown. Evaluation consistency is 40% or, at extra cost, 50%; there is no funded consistency rule. Funded contract scaling applies and can change after withdrawals.
- **Payout:** five qualifying days per cycle, each above the 50K \$150 threshold; payout is 50% of profit, capped at \$2,000 per request and with a \$500 minimum. Five published requests give at most \$9,000 after the 90% split at 50K.
- **Advantages:** cheapest EOD Lucid evaluation at the 50K baseline, no fixed funded payout buffer, no funded consistency rule, and a payout request locks the floor under the documented Flex rules.
- **Issues:** 50K cannot deliver \$10,000 in hand from those five simulated payouts alone. Smaller extraction trades must still meet qualifying-day thresholds. The source does not resolve whether 50% means current account profit or profit since the last payout; this substantially changes an accumulated-profit exit. A 100K DLL-on / 40% evaluation costs \$150.50, has a \$3,000 drawdown and \$6,000 target, and its five \$2,500 caps allow at most \$11,250 after split, subject to the same uncertainties.

### 4.3 Lucid Daily

- **Purchase and risk:** 50K EOD evaluation / intraday funded, DLL on / 40% costs \$85.40. Captured EOD in both phases with DLL off / 40% costs \$235.20; the EOD-funded / DLL-on price and funded card are missing. Initial drawdown is \$2,000; funded drawdown type is selected at purchase. The documented funded DLL is \$1,200 or off.
- **Payout:** daily eligibility, no funded consistency rule, no stated per-request cap, and profit above the \$52,100 buffer may be requested, subject to the \$500 minimum and other conditions. A DLL-off configuration loses on-demand intraday payouts.
- **Advantages:** comparatively flexible extraction of retained profit; no 50% formula; EOD-funded choice exists on captured cards.
- **Issues:** funded US high-impact news restrictions are a hard breach, conflicting with the stated accidental-news requirement. Intraday trailing can cost cushion before the floor locks. EOD-funded pricing and conditions are incompletely captured, and the help centre conflicts with the cards about funded drawdown. A maximum daily profit trigger and separate live-transition terms also matter; do not assume unlimited withdrawals are guaranteed.

### 4.4 Lucid Direct

- **Purchase and risk:** \$360.50 for 50K straight to funded; \$2,000 EOD drawdown; \$1,200 DLL converting to LucidScale after the trail phase. No evaluation or funded scaling plan.
- **Payout:** 20% consistency and at least five trading days; first cycle profit goal \$3,000, later goals \$2,500, resetting after payout. Published caps are \$2,000 for payouts 1–3 and \$2,500 for 4–5.
- **Advantages:** avoids an evaluation and its target; EOD drawdown, no documented funded news restriction and 30-day inactivity.
- **Issues:** high purchase cost, strict consistency and resetting profit goals can delay withdrawal even with a large existing balance. The fifth-versus-sixth payout documentation conflict, potential live move and unknown reset availability prevent a confident single-account ceiling. The first three caps total \$6,000 gross, or \$5,400 after split, if all are achieved without an earlier transition.

## 5. Tradeify products

For Growth, Select and Lightning, Tradeify documents a weekly trade requirement. Live review can begin after three payouts on one account, but that is **consideration, not an automatic transition**. Growth and Lightning policies also reserve the right to move the trader after any successful payout; the extent and timing of discretionary moves for each product need confirmation. Live-account risk and remaining simulated-profit treatment are incompletely documented.

These funded products also have a **10-second payout rule**: more than 50% of trades must last longer than 10 seconds, and more than 50% of profit must come from trades lasting longer than 10 seconds. Failing either condition blocks payouts until the ratios recover. The stated minutes-long trading style should normally fit, but the account's actual trades and profits determine eligibility. Whether this rule applies to Inter Miami is not documented.

### 5.1 Growth

- **Purchase and risk:** 50K evaluation \$87, reset \$95, \$3,000 target, no evaluation consistency rule. EOD drawdown is \$2,000 in both phases. DLL starts at \$1,250 and rises to \$2,000 at the stated funded threshold. No funded scaling plan.
- **Payout:** five qualifying days with at least the documented \$150 daily profit threshold, 35% consistency and a \$53,000 minimum balance to request. Caps progress \$1,500, \$2,000, \$2,500, then \$3,000 per request. The first three total \$6,000 gross or \$5,400 at 90%, if fully achieved.
- **Advantages:** low price, straightforward evaluation and defined initial funded DLL; no 50% payout formula.
- **Issues:** consistency can bind after large winning days, payout cycles require qualifying profits and the weekly rule reduces scheduling flexibility. Three payouts reach the review threshold; a discretionary move may occur even earlier. The \$100K costs \$153 and offers \$3,500 drawdown, but has a \$6,000 evaluation target and different payout thresholds.

### 5.2 Select Flex

- **Purchase and risk:** 50K Select evaluation \$116 with 40% consistency, \$3,000 target and \$2,000 EOD trailing drawdown. Flex is chosen at funded activation and has no DLL. Funded contract scaling applies.
- **Payout:** five qualifying winning days, 50% of total profit, \$2,500 cap per request, \$250 minimum and no funded consistency rule or fixed payout buffer. The 50% evaluation consistency add-on reduces the 50K Flex payout cap to \$2,000 in the captured rules; its price is uncertain because purchase-page and pricing-reference descriptions differ.
- **Advantages:** no fixed payout buffer, no funded consistency, and a larger per-request cap than Select Daily at 50K.
- **Issues:** the 50% formula limits each withdrawal, and the account has no DLL to halt a losing session. Five winning days can be slow at the stated frequency, and a payout can change the applicable contract tier. Weekly activity and possible live transition remain material. Three maximum baseline requests would total \$7,500 gross or \$6,750 after split, but review can occur before or after this and payout conditions still apply.

### 5.3 Select Daily

- **Purchase and risk:** same 50K Select evaluation at \$116; choose Daily at activation. Funded drawdown is \$2,000 EOD and DLL \$1,000. Funded scaling applies; the 100K and 150K Daily drawdowns are smaller than their Flex counterparts.
- **Payout:** daily eligibility, no funded consistency, \$52,100 buffer, up to twice net profit since the last payout, capped at \$1,250 per request, with a \$250 minimum. A cycle must be net positive and only one request can be open at a time.
- **Advantages:** can withdraw accumulated profits without a 50%-of-current-profit restriction, while trading smaller to generate the required new cycle profit. The EOD floor and DLL suit controlled growth.
- **Issues:** the multiplier does not create profit: the balance must remain above the buffer. At least five capped requests are needed for \$5,000 in hand and nine for \$10,000, before fees and tax. This is arithmetic, not a feasible schedule; fresh cycle profit, processing and possible live transition may require longer or prevent completion. The weekly inactivity requirement also applies.

### 5.4 Lightning

- **Purchase and risk:** \$344 for 50K straight to funded; \$2,000 EOD drawdown, \$1,250 DLL, full documented contract size and no reset.
- **Payout:** 20%, 25%, then 30% consistency across successive payout stages; \$3,000 first-cycle goal, \$2,000 on later cycles, and caps of \$2,000 for payouts 1–3 then \$2,500. Cycle profit goals reset; minimum payout is \$1,000.
- **Advantages:** no evaluation; no funded scaling plan; more permissive consistency after the first payout.
- **Issues:** expensive, no reset, weekly inactivity, payout goals and consistency can delay access to retained profits. Source documents disagree between no minimum trading-day count and a five-day payout frequency. Live transition can occur after a successful payout. Three maximum requests are \$6,000 gross or \$5,400 after split, subject to all conditions and transition risk.

### 5.5 Inter Miami

- **Purchase and risk:** 50K evaluation \$263 with soft \$900 DLL or \$440 with no DLL; \$3,500 target; \$1,500 static drawdown. Tradovate only, maximum 30 micros.
- **Payout:** cards say daily frequency, \$600 minimum gross payout and 80% trader share, with a path to live after \$12,000 in payouts. The meaning of the threshold and its interaction with the split should be confirmed.
- **Advantages:** static floor allows retained profit to increase the cushion without first waiting for a trailing-floor lock. It matches the preferred platform and supports MNQ.
- **Issues:** payout formula, maximum payout, consistency, inactivity, reset price and application of general rules are not documented. The smaller initial drawdown and 30-micro maximum constrain risk and size. At 80%, \$10,000 in hand needs \$12,500 gross, so the \$12,000 live-transition figure is potentially decisive but cannot be interpreted without clarification.

## 8. Most important / restrictive rules

This section isolates rules that either make an evaluation harder to pass or make funded profit harder to withdraw. Rules are listed only for the products to which the captured documents apply. A rule can be a delay rather than a permanent bar: for example, consistency may require continued profitable trading until the largest day is diluted.

### 8.1 Evaluation stage: rules that raise breach or failure risk

| Rule | Why it matters for passing | Applicable accounts |
|---|---|---|
| **Trailing drawdown during the evaluation** | As the balance rises, the floor follows the applicable peak until its lock point, reducing room for a later loss. Tradeify evaluates its trailing floor from end-of-day highs but enforces it in real time; an open trade can breach before a stop or session close. Lucid EOD floors follow closing balances. | All Lucid Pro, Flex and Daily evaluations; Tradeify Growth and Select evaluations. Lucid Daily can be bought with intraday evaluation drawdown, where unrealised peaks can raise the floor during the session. |
| **Intraday evaluation drawdown** | A profitable open excursion that reverses can permanently raise the floor and consume room without a closed profit. | Lucid Daily evaluations configured for intraday drawdown. |
| **No daily loss limit** | There is no separate daily lockout before the trailing floor is hit, making a multi-trade losing session more likely to breach the account. | Tradeify Select 25K–150K evaluations; Lucid evaluations configured with DLL off. |
| **DLL is not the whole drawdown allowance** | A DLL can halt a session, but if the remaining drawdown is closer, the account can breach first. A soft/lock behavior also does not absorb slippage or guarantee survival. | Lucid Pro, Flex and Daily evaluations with DLL on; Tradeify Growth evaluations; Tradeify Inter Miami with soft DLL. |
| **Evaluation consistency** | A strong single day can exceed the allowed share of total profit. The trader must generate more profit or additional days before passing, continuing exposure after the target may already have been reached. | Lucid Flex (40% or 50%); Lucid Daily (tiers depend on drawdown configuration); Tradeify Select (40%, or 50% add-on). |
| **Minimum trading days** | Even after hitting the target, the evaluation remains open and exposed until the minimum is met. | Tradeify Select: 3 days at 40%, 2 at 50%; Lucid Daily: 2–4 depending on settings. Lucid Pro and Tradeify Growth have no multi-day minimum in the captured rules. |
| **Static but smaller drawdown** | Static avoids a moving floor, but the Inter Miami 50K starts with only \$1,500 of drawdown. Its soft \$900 DLL is not much room for several wide-stop losses. | Tradeify Inter Miami evaluation. |

**Product-specific pass exposure:** Lucid Pro has no evaluation consistency hurdle, and Tradeify Growth has both no evaluation consistency rule and a DLL. Tradeify Select has no DLL at these sizes and adds consistency and minimum days. Lucid Daily's intraday evaluation option is the most sensitive to unrealised reversals; choose EOD evaluation if using Daily and avoiding that mechanic is important. Inter Miami's static floor is simpler, but its initial room is smaller and its complete evaluation enforcement rules are not documented.

No evaluation rule guarantees that an account will fail or pass. Stop width, position size, remaining room, slippage and the sequence of trades determine the actual breach risk.

**No evaluation phase:** Lucid Direct and Tradeify Lightning begin under funded rules. They avoid evaluation-pass hurdles, but the funded-stage payout and account-risk restrictions below apply from the first trade.

| Rule | Why it matters | Applicable accounts |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily Loss Limit (DLL)** | The DLL is a per-session loss budget, not a per-trade cap. It can nevertheless constrain per-trade risk and position size: one oversized loss may hit it, or multiple trades may use the allowance and stop further trading that day. At 50K with a 100-point stop, one MNQ risks about \$200 before costs; a \$1,000 DLL is five such planned losses, while a \$1,250 DLL is six with \$50 left. | Rule | Why it adds time or exposure | Applicable accounts |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualifying / winning days per cycle** | A profitable balance alone may not qualify for payout until the required days meet their thresholds. Smaller trades reduce risk but may not produce a qualifying day. | Lucid Pro: 3 trading days; Lucid Flex: 5 qualifying days (50K threshold above \$150); Tradeify Growth: 5 qualifying days (50K threshold above \$150); Select Flex: 5 winning days; Select Daily: daily eligibility, but the cycle must be net positive. Lucid Direct requires at least 5 trading days; Tradeify Lightning cadence is disputed between no minimum and 5 days. |
| **Payout consistency** | A large winning day can make a request ineligible until further profits dilute it, forcing continued trading and exposure. | Lucid Pro: 40%; Direct: 20%; Tradeify Growth: 35%; Lightning: 20% / 25% / 30% for payouts 1 / 2 / 3+. No funded consistency rule on Lucid Flex / Daily or Tradeify Select Flex / Daily. Inter Miami is unknown. |
| **Profit goal resets after each payout** | Profit left in the account does not count toward the next goal; new qualifying profit must be generated before the next request. | Lucid Direct and Tradeify Lightning. |
| **Minimum new profit per cycle** | A high accumulated balance is not sufficient if each request requires new profit after the previous payout. | Tradeify Select Daily requires a net-positive cycle and pays up to 2× profit since the previous payout. Lucid Direct sets a new profit goal each cycle; Tradeify Lightning resets its cycle goal. |
| **10-second payout gate** | More than 50% of trades must last longer than 10 seconds **and** more than 50% of profit must come from trades lasting longer than 10 seconds. Failing either test blocks a payout until the ratios recover. | Tradeify Growth, Select Flex, Select Daily and Lightning. The usual minutes-long trades should normally meet the duration condition; the profit share test still applies. Inter Miami applicability is undocumented. |

The funded DLL applies in parallel with funded drawdown and the payout requirements above. The caps below show only the mechanical ceiling from published per-request limits; they do not prove that the account can survive or complete the required number of payout cycles.

#### Published cap ceilings that can decide the target

These are gross cap arithmetic after the stated 90% split, before fees and tax. They assume all listed payouts are available and approved; they do not account for an earlier transition or prove that the required profits and eligibility conditions will be met.

| 50K funded product | Published cap arithmetic | Maximum net from those requests | Consequence for target |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Lucid Pro | \$2,000 + four \$2,500 requests | \$10,800 | Both targets fit narrowly; \$10K needs all five |
| Lucid Flex | Five \$2,000 requests | \$9,000 | \$5K fits; \$10K does not on the five documented payouts |
| Lucid Direct | Three \$2,000 + two \$2,500 requests | \$9,900 | \$5K fits; \$10K misses by \$100 before fees; sixth payout is unclear |
| Tradeify Growth | First three caps: \$1,500 + \$2,000 + \$2,500 | \$5,400 | \$5K can fit within three payouts; subsequent cap is \$3,000, but live movement can intervene |
| Tradeify Select Flex | \$2,500 per request | No fixed total cap documented | Per-request formula and duration gate continue to apply |
| Tradeify Select Daily | \$1,250 per request | No fixed total cap documented | At least 5 max requests for \$5K net; 9 for \$10K net |
| Tradeify Lightning | \$2,000 for payouts 1–3, then \$2,500 | First 3 yield \$5,400 net | \$5K fits within 3 capped requests; \$10K needs at least 6 requests, subject to goals and transition |
| Tradeify Inter Miami | Maximum not published | Unknown | Cannot assess either target |

**Selection implication:** the strongest extraction formula is not necessarily the easiest account to live with. Lucid Pro's caps can support \$10,000 in hand but need five successful payouts and consistency. Tradeify Select Daily has no 50% current-profit formula, but its \$1,250 cap requires many requests and new profit; weekly inactivity and live-transition discretion add exposure. Lucid Flex is inexpensive and has no funded consistency rule, but the 50% formula and five-payout ceiling limit the 50K outcome. Products with missing payout rules cannot be treated as passing either target.
