- trading frequency: at least one trade a week!
- End-of-day trailing max drawdown: so the max drawdown amount is only updated at the end of the day, so a peak in account balance intra-day does not move it up. Only the closing balance at the end of the day
- Max drawdown is enforced also intra-day! so even though it is not moving, the intra-day balance will fail the account if it goes below the max drawdown.
- On most plans the max draw down locks after a certain threshold on the funded accounts

- On the accounts that have a DLL: it increases after a 6% profit in the account to the same value as the max drawdown.

Select - Flex:
- Has contract size scaling plan
- Only plan without a daily loss limit --> beneficial for later profit scaling / increasing
- Has max 50% of profits can be requested for payout. Does this harm the potential profitability?
- Also has a max payout in dollars (different per account size)
- The 50% profit max becomes irrelevant as soon as you have more profits than the max payout cap. So if you have a lot of profits (above the cap) you need multiple payouts to extract the money. But this is limited by the number of days per payout cycle! You need to have minimum of 5 days with $x dollars profit. So there is a bit of a limit here...

Select - Daily:
- Significantly lower layout cap compared to Select - flex.
- Daily Loss Limit!! Limits profit per day --> limits scaling a little bit (for a single account)
- No consistency rule, and no required results between payouts. So easiest to request multiple payouts after a lot of profits.


Lightning
