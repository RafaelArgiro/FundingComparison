# Tradeify — Live Phase

Rules that apply once an account is moved to Tradeify Elite Live: real capital, real
markets.

> **Read `Tr_Rules_General.md` first.** Trading hours, prohibited and permitted conduct,
> instruments and platforms apply here and are not repeated.

> **Major data gap.** The **Tradeify Elite Program** article is not in the captured
> material, and neither is **Sim and Live Account Restrictions**. Both are referenced
> repeatedly as the authoritative source for this phase. Everything below is assembled
> from passing mentions in the evaluation, funded, payout and conduct articles. The core
> risk parameters of a live account — drawdown, contract limits, daily loss limit, payout
> caps — are **not published anywhere in this material**. Treat this document as
> provisional and see section 8 for the full list of what is missing.

Consequence codes:
- **KILL** account lost immediately
- **LOCK** trading halted until next session
- **BLOCK** payout blocked or denied
- **STRIP** profits removed
- **BAN** consequences beyond this account

---

## 1. What is specific to the live phase

| | |
|---|---|
| Capital | **Real**, not simulated |
| Profit split | **80% / 20%** — down from 90/10 when funded |
| Payout frequency | Daily |
| Platform access | The Tradovate API becomes available; it is blocked on evaluation and sim funded accounts |
| Simulated accounts | **Cannot be held at the same time** |
| Entry | Controlled by Tradeify, not by you |
| Exit | A denied application means **no return to simulated trading** |

**The split drop is a 11% cut in take-home.** The same gross profit that paid \$900 per
\$1,000 when funded pays \$800 live. Legacy Live accounts keep their original split terms,
but the Legacy Live programme itself is not described anywhere in this material.

**Live traders may be copied by the firm.** The microscalping rationale states Tradeify
wants traders "that can be called up to live to eventually be copied by our own firm as
well". This is the stated reason the 10-second rule exists, and it implies live trading is
expected to be replicable.

---

## 2. Getting there

**Eligibility thresholds.** You become eligible to be *considered* once you meet **one** of:

- **3 payouts on a single account**, or
- **10 total payouts** since your last live transition, across all plan types.

Measured since your last live transition, or since your first purchase if you have never
transitioned before. The thresholds are identical for Growth, Select and Lightning.

**Meeting the threshold does not qualify you.** The documentation is explicit: these are
"minimum requirements for consideration, not automatic qualification", and "most traders
will not be transitioned to live immediately upon hitting these thresholds". Tradeify
evaluates holistically on consistency, risk management and overall trading behaviour, and
contacts the trader directly when selected.

**Tradeify can also move you unilaterally.** Both the Growth and Lightning payout policies
state that Tradeify "reserves the right to move you to a Live Funded Account at any time
after a successful payout", notified by email. The trigger is a single successful payout,
not the thresholds above.

**One live account per qualifying funded account.** Each funded account that has received
at least one payout can transition to its own Elite Live account. A funded account with
zero payouts does not qualify.

---

## 3. The one-way door

This is the structural feature that matters most, and it is the part least covered by the
available material.

- **You cannot hold simulated and live accounts at the same time.** Once live, you may not
  start new evaluations.
- **A denied live application cannot return to simulated trading.** Stated plainly in the
  restricted-countries article: "If your application for a Live Account is denied, you will
  not be permitted to return to the Simulated stage."
- **Holding a live account blocks Funded Reset**, and so does anyone in your household
  holding one. The stated reason is that you cannot "buy back into simulated trading while
  you hold a real-money Live account".
- **The transition is not described as optional.** No opt-out, deferral or cash-out
  mechanism appears anywhere in the captured material.

Taken together: reaching live is controlled by Tradeify, refusing it is undocumented, and
failing the application removes the simulated route permanently. What happens to your
other funded accounts, and whether their balances are paid out, forfeited or carried, is
not stated.

## 4. Rules confirmed to carry over

These are explicitly stated to apply to Elite Live, not merely assumed:

- **Trading hours.** Flat by 4:45 PM ET, 12:59 PM ET on holiday half-days. No overnight,
  no weekend, no swing trading. The permitted-times article names Live explicitly and the
  FAQ confirms it: "the no overnight positions rule applies to ALL Tradeify account types,
  including Live accounts."
- **Hedging.** BAN. The hedging article states it applies to "All Tradeify account types
  (Evaluation, Sim Funded, and Elite Live)", and automated hedging detection runs on Elite
  Live accounts under the same three-condition test.
- **Restricted countries.** Residency-based eligibility applies, and live applications are
  additionally screened.

Everything else in `Tr_Rules_General.md` is presumed to carry over but is not confirmed
for Live specifically.

## 5. Account type notes

**Growth, Select and Lightning all lead to the same Elite Live programme.** The thresholds,
split and mechanics are identical regardless of which product you came from, and the
Select Flex versus Select Daily choice does not change the live path.

**Inter Miami runs a different route.** Its plan cards state:

- **Live account after \$12,000 in payouts**, rather than the 3-or-10 payout thresholds.
- Profit split **80% / 10% / 10%**, described as "80% profit split + 10% to Live Funds".
  The third 10% appears to pre-fund the live account, but the mechanism is not explained
  and the destination is never stated.

Nothing else is known about the Inter Miami live path, including whether it joins the same
Elite Live programme or is separate.

---

## 6. What takes the account away

**Unknown.** The live drawdown model, its size, and whether it is trailing or static are
not published anywhere in this material. Neither is the daily loss limit, if one exists.

What can be said:

- **Hedging** remains a BAN-level violation with automated detection.
- **Holding a position past 4:45 PM ET** is not permitted, though whether live accounts are
  auto-flattened without penalty, as simulated accounts are, is not stated.
- **A denied application** — which is an entry condition rather than a breach — permanently
  removes the simulated route.

Whether a live breach ends the relationship, permits a return to simulated trading, or
carries a cooldown is **not documented**.

---

## 7. What limits your profit

Only one limiter is confirmed:

1. **The profit split falls from 90% to 80%.** For identical gross performance, net income
   drops by 11%. This is the single confirmed economic change on moving live.

Everything else that would determine live profitability is unpublished: payout minimums
and maximums, whether caps scale with payout number, whether a consistency rule applies,
whether the 10-second rule gates live payouts, contract limits, and how much capital is
actually allocated.

**Daily payouts are confirmed**, which is a faster cadence than any simulated product
except Select Daily and Inter Miami. Without the cap figures this cannot be converted into
a throughput number.

## 8. What is not published

The following would all need to be obtained before this phase can be assessed:

**Risk parameters**
- Live drawdown: amount, type (trailing or static), and enforcement basis
- Whether the drawdown locks, and at what threshold
- Daily loss limit, if any
- Contract limits, and whether a scaling plan applies

**Capital**
- How much real capital is allocated, and on what basis
- Whether simulated profit transfers, is capped, or is forfeited
- What happens to the balances of other funded accounts at transition

**Payouts**
- Minimum and maximum payout per request
- Whether caps progress with payout number
- Whether a consistency rule applies
- Whether the 10-second rule gates live payouts
- Processing times and methods

**Lifecycle**
- Whether the transition can be refused or deferred
- What happens on a live breach
- Whether any cooldown or return path exists
- Whether the Legacy Live programme is still open, and to whom
- Commission and fee schedule on live, and whether it differs from simulated

**Inter Miami**
- Whether its \$12,000 route leads to the same programme
- Where the third 10% of the split goes, and whether it is recoverable
