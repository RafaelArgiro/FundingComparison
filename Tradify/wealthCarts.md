WealthCharts Platform Overview
Complete guide to getting started with WealthCharts, a web-based trading platform with 200+ indicators, advanced DOM, Trade Copier, WealthTracker analytics, and mobile app support.

August 5, 2026
This guide covers everything you need to know about getting started with WealthCharts, a web-based futures trading platform available to Tradeify customers. WealthCharts offers advanced charting, a full-featured trading DOM, Trade Copier functionality, and comprehensive performance analytics through WealthTracker.



⚠️ Important: WealthCharts uses separate login credentials
WealthCharts is one of three broker/platform options Tradeify offers (alongside Tradovate and Rithmic), all at the same price with no surcharge. WealthCharts is operated by a third party and uses its own login credentials — separate from your Tradeify/Tradovate credentials. Your WealthCharts username and password are provided in your Tradeify dashboard once you select WealthCharts at checkout. WealthCharts is not required to trade with Tradeify — you can select Tradovate or Rithmic at checkout instead.





Platform Overview
WealthCharts is a web-based trading and charting platform designed for active futures traders. Key advantages include:

No Downloads Required - Fully web-based platform accessible from any modern browser

200+ Technical Indicators - Exclusive, Premier, and Standard indicator categories

Advanced Trading DOM - Full-featured price ladder with one-click order entry (Level 1 data)

Trade Copier - Mirror trades across multiple accounts simultaneously

WealthTracker Analytics - Built-in performance tracking, trade journal, and P&L analysis

Mobile App - Trade on iOS and Android with the same credentials



Level 1 data: WealthCharts accounts include Level 1 CME market data. The DOM shows the best bid and best ask along with your own working orders — it does not show full order book depth. Level 2 depth data and EUREX data are not yet available on WealthCharts accounts and cannot be purchased — coming soon. See Level 2 (L2) and EUREX Data: Availability by Broker.





Getting Started


Accessing WealthCharts
Your WealthCharts credentials are provided by Tradeify. To find them:

Log in to your Tradeify dashboard

Locate your WealthCharts username and password at the top of the dashboard

Go to the WealthCharts login page and enter your credentials

Copy and paste your credentials directly to avoid typing errors



Connecting Your Trading Account
After logging in, you need to connect your trading account to receive market data and place trades:

Open the Main Menu (top-left)

Navigate to Broker and Data Sources

Select your broker connection and enter your credentials

Choose the Demo/Simulation environment (never select Live for Tradeify accounts)

Verify the connection status shows as connected



Verifying Market Data
Once connected, confirm your data feed is active:

Open a chart for a supported instrument (e.g., ES or NQ)

Verify price data is updating in real time

Check that the DOM shows live bid/ask prices

If data appears delayed or missing, disconnect and reconnect your broker connection





Workspace & Layout


Dashboard Overview
The WealthCharts interface is organized into several main areas:

Main Menu - Access all platform features, settings, and tools from the top-left menu

Toolbar - Quick access to charts, DOM, indicators, drawing tools, and other frequently used features

Chart Area - The central workspace displaying your charts and trading panels

Sidebar - Watchlists, alerts, and quick-access panels



Customizing Your Layout
WealthCharts allows you to fully customize your workspace:

Add or remove panels by using the toolbar or Main Menu

Drag and resize panels to arrange your ideal layout

Save your layout through Main Menu so it persists across sessions

Use layout templates to quickly switch between different trading setups



Symbol Linking
Symbol linking connects multiple panels (charts, DOM, watchlists) to the same instrument. When you change the symbol in one linked panel, all other panels with the same link color update automatically. Use the colored link icon in each panel's toolbar to assign a link group.





Symbol Selection
WealthCharts uses standard CME futures symbol conventions:

Front-month contracts - Use the standard format: symbol + month code + year digit (e.g., ESH6 for March 2026 E-mini S&P 500)

Continuous contracts - Use the symbol followed by "1" (e.g., ES1 for continuous E-mini S&P 500). Continuous contracts automatically roll to the next front-month contract.

Common symbols - ES (S&P 500), NQ (NASDAQ), YM (Dow), CL (Crude Oil), GC (Gold), NKD (Nikkei)

Use the symbol search bar at the top of any chart to find instruments. Type the symbol name or description and select from the results.





Multi-Device Access


Web Platform
WealthCharts runs entirely in your web browser with no downloads or installations required. Supported browsers include Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, use an up-to-date browser with a stable internet connection.



Mobile App
WealthCharts offers a mobile app for iOS and Android:

Download from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store

Log in with the same credentials you use on the web platform

Access charting, order entry via DOM, and account monitoring on the go

View positions, orders, and account balances from your mobile device





Platform Resources
WealthCharts Knowledge Base - https://www.wealthcharts.com/kb for detailed platform documentation and tutorials

Tradeify Support - Contact Tradeify support through your dashboard for account or credential issues

WealthCharts Charting Guide
Complete guide to charting in WealthCharts, covering chart creation, bar styles, timeframes, 200+ indicators, drawing tools, chart settings, templates, Chart Replay, and the Data Window.

July 29, 2026
WealthCharts provides a comprehensive charting suite with 200+ technical indicators, multiple bar styles, customizable timeframes, drawing tools, and Chart Replay for practice. This guide covers all charting features available on the platform.





Creating & Managing Charts
To open a new chart:

Click the chart icon in the toolbar, or use Main Menu and select New Chart

Enter a symbol in the search bar (e.g., ES1, NQ1, CL1)

Select the desired timeframe from the timeframe selector

The chart opens in your current workspace and can be repositioned or resized

You can open multiple charts simultaneously and arrange them in your workspace. Use the chart toolbar at the top of each chart panel to access symbol search, timeframes, bar styles, indicators, and drawing tools.





Bar Styles
WealthCharts supports multiple chart bar styles. Change the style using the bar style dropdown in the chart toolbar:

Candlestick - Traditional OHLC candles with color-coded bodies (default)

OHLC Bars - Open-High-Low-Close bar chart

Line - Simple line connecting closing prices

Area - Line chart with filled area below

Heikin Ashi - Smoothed candlesticks that filter market noise

Kagi - Direction-based chart that ignores time

Renko - Brick-based chart focused on price movement





Timeframes & Intervals


Standard Timeframes
WealthCharts offers a full range of standard timeframes:

Minute charts - 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60 minute intervals

Hourly charts - 1, 2, 4 hour intervals

Daily, Weekly, Monthly - Longer-term analysis



Custom Timeframes
You can create unlimited custom timeframes. Click the timeframe selector and choose a custom interval to define any combination of ticks, minutes, hours, or days that suits your trading strategy.





Technical Indicators


Adding Indicators
WealthCharts offers 200+ technical indicators organized into three categories:

Exclusive Indicators - Proprietary indicators unique to WealthCharts

Premier Indicators - Premium indicators with advanced analysis capabilities

Standard Indicators - Classic technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages, Bollinger Bands, etc.)

To add an indicator, click the Indicators button in the chart toolbar and browse or search for the indicator you want. Select it to add it to your current chart.



Configuring Indicators
After adding an indicator, you can configure its parameters, visual style, and colors. Click on the indicator name or use Configure Chart to access settings. You can also set alerts based on indicator values.



Removing Indicators
To remove an indicator, open Configure Chart from the chart toolbar. Find the indicator in the list and click the delete button next to it.





Drawing Tools
WealthCharts includes 30+ drawing tools organized into six categories:

Lines - Trend lines, horizontal lines, vertical lines, rays, channels

Shapes - Rectangles, circles, triangles

Fibonacci - Retracement, extension, fan, time zones

Text - Text labels and annotations

Freehand - Freehand drawing tool for custom markups

Measuring - Price range, bar count, and percentage measurement tools

Access drawing tools from the toolbar. Click a tool to activate it, then click and drag on the chart to draw. Right-click a drawing to edit its properties or delete it.





Chart Settings


Configure Chart
Open Configure Chart from the chart toolbar to customize:

Background color and grid display

Candle/bar colors for bullish and bearish movements

Subchart layout for indicators displayed below the main chart

Indicator parameters and visual settings



Chart Templates
Save your chart configuration as a template to quickly apply the same indicators, styles, and settings to new charts. Templates save your bar style, timeframe, indicators, and color settings. You can set a default template that applies to all new charts.



Global Chart Settings
Global settings apply across all charts and include:

Crosshair style and behavior

Last price line visibility

Extended hours data display

Font size and style preferences





Chart Replay
Chart Replay lets you rewind any chart to a past date and replay price action at adjustable speeds. This is useful for:

Practicing trade setups with historical data

Reviewing past market events

Testing indicator configurations against real price action

Building pattern recognition skills without risking capital

To use Chart Replay, click the Replay button in the chart toolbar, select a start date, and use the playback controls to play, pause, and adjust speed.





Data Window
The Data Window displays detailed OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) values and indicator readings for the bar under your cursor. As you move your mouse across the chart, the Data Window updates in real time. This is helpful for seeing exact price levels and indicator values at any point in time.

WealthCharts Order Entry & Trade Copier
Complete guide to order entry in WealthCharts, covering the Trading DOM, market/limit/stop orders, bracket orders, on-chart trading, position management, and Trade Copier setup with important rules and limitations.

July 21, 2026
This guide covers all order entry features in WealthCharts, including the Trading DOM, order types, bracket orders, on-chart trading, position management, and the Trade Copier for mirroring trades across multiple accounts.



Important Copy Trading Rules
Copy trading (using Trade Copier or any method) is allowed on Tradeify, but you may only copy trades between accounts that you personally own and manage. Copying trades from or to accounts owned by other people is strictly prohibited and may result in account termination. All copied accounts must trade in the same direction — a copier configured to invert direction, or a lead account flattened while followers stay open, creates opposing positions across accounts and is a hedging violation. See Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products.





Trading DOM
The Trading DOM (Depth of Market) is the primary order entry tool in WealthCharts. It displays a vertical price ladder showing:

Price ladder - Vertical list of price levels for the selected instrument

Bid/Ask columns - Number of contracts available at each price level

Your orders - Working orders displayed at their price levels

Position info - Current position quantity and average entry price

P&L display - Real-time profit/loss for your open position

The DOM includes lock and auto-center controls. Lock keeps the price ladder fixed in place, while auto-center scrolls the ladder to keep the current price visible.





Order Types


Market Orders
Market orders execute immediately at the best available price:

Buy Mkt / Sell Mkt - Click the Buy Market or Sell Market buttons at the bottom of the DOM

Join Bid / Join Ask - Place a limit order at the current best bid or ask price for potential price improvement



Limit & Stop Orders
Place limit and stop orders directly on the DOM price ladder:

Limit orders - Click on the bid column (buy) or ask column (sell) at your desired price level

Stop orders - Right-click on the price ladder to place stop orders at specific levels

Adjusting orders - Drag working orders up or down the price ladder to modify the price



Time in Force
GTC (Good Till Cancelled) - Order remains active until filled or manually cancelled

Day - Order expires at the end of the current trading session





Bracket Orders


Simple Bracket
A bracket order automatically attaches a take-profit and stop-loss to your entry order:

Set your take-profit target (number of ticks/points above or below entry)

Set your stop-loss level (number of ticks/points for risk)

Optionally enable trailing stop to follow price in your favor

Optionally enable breakeven stop to move stop to entry after reaching a specified profit



Multi-Bracket Orders
Multi-bracket orders let you set multiple take-profit levels for scaling out of a position. For example, you can take profit on half your position at one target and let the rest run to a second target. You can save bracket configurations as strategies for quick reuse.





On-Chart Trading


Order Panel
WealthCharts allows you to trade directly from the chart using the built-in order panel. Enable it from the chart toolbar to see buy/sell buttons and order quantity controls overlaid on your chart.



Right-Click Order Entry
Right-click anywhere on the chart to access a context menu with order options. You can place buy or sell orders at the specific price level where you right-clicked, including limit orders, stop orders, and bracket orders.





Position Management
WealthCharts provides quick-action buttons for managing open positions:

Flatten - Close your entire position at market price

Reverse - Close your current position and open an equal position in the opposite direction

Cancel All - Cancel all working orders for the current instrument

Scaling - Add to or reduce your position size. Positions follow FIFO (First In, First Out) order.





Trade Copier


What is Trade Copier
Trade Copier mirrors trades across multiple accounts simultaneously. When you place a trade on one account, it is automatically replicated on all other selected accounts. This is equivalent to Tradovate's Group Trading feature.



Setting Up Trade Copier
To configure Trade Copier:

Open Main Menu and navigate to Trade Copier

Create a new strategy or select an existing one

Check the accounts you want to include in the copy group

Set the ratio for each account (e.g., 1:1 to copy the same quantity, or 1:2 to double)

Configure cross settings if trading between mini and micro contracts

Select the symbols to copy

Activate the strategy



Important Rules & Limitations
Session-based - Trade Copier must be re-activated each session. It does not persist when you log out.

No duplicate symbols - Each symbol can only appear in one active Trade Copier strategy at a time

Flatten behavior - When you flatten a position on the lead account, all copied accounts flatten as well

Only your accounts - You may only copy trades between accounts you personally own and manage

WealthCharts Risk & Account Settings
Complete guide to account monitoring, risk management, and contract rollovers in WealthCharts. Learn how to use Broker Portfolio, Liquidation Indicator, User Set Risk, and manage contract rollovers.

February 22, 2026
This guide covers account monitoring and risk management in WealthCharts, including the Broker Portfolio, Liquidation Indicator, User Set Risk parameters, and contract rollover procedures.





Broker Portfolio
The Broker Portfolio panel provides a comprehensive view of your trading accounts. Access it from the toolbar or Main Menu. It contains four tabs:



Open Positions Tab
Displays all currently open positions across your accounts:

Symbol and contract name

Quantity and direction (long/short)

Cost basis (average entry price)

Current market price

Unrealized P&L



Orders Tab
Shows all working and completed orders:

Order type (market, limit, stop)

Status (working, filled, cancelled, rejected)

Fill prices and quantities

Time of execution



Trades Tab
Lists past trades with detailed P&L breakdown, including entry and exit prices, trade duration, and realized profit or loss for each trade.



Accounts Tab
Displays key account metrics for all connected accounts:

Balance - Current account balance

Max Balance - Highest balance reached (high-water mark)

Net Liquidity - Balance including unrealized P&L from open positions

Liquidation Threshold - The trailing drawdown level where auto-liquidation occurs

Drawdown - Distance between current net liquidity and liquidation threshold

Daily P&L - Profit or loss for the current trading day





Liquidation Indicator
The Liquidation Indicator is a visual overlay that displays your trailing drawdown threshold directly on the chart. It shows a horizontal line at the price level where your account would be auto-liquidated, making it easy to see how much room you have before hitting the drawdown limit.

To enable the Liquidation Indicator, open Configure Chart or the Indicators menu and search for "Liquidation Indicator." Add it to your chart and it will display in real time as your drawdown threshold changes.





User Set Risk


Configuring Risk Parameters
WealthCharts allows you to set personal risk controls on a per-account basis. These are in addition to Tradeify's account-level risk rules (Daily Loss Limit and Max Trailing Drawdown).

To configure risk settings:

Open Broker Portfolio and go to the Accounts tab

Click the pencil/edit icon next to the account you want to configure

Set your desired risk parameters (max daily loss, max position size, etc.)

Save your settings



Suspended Account Behavior
If your User Set Risk triggers and suspends trading, your risk settings can still be modified. The suspension is lifted automatically at the start of the next trading session. You can adjust your risk parameters while the account is suspended to prevent the same trigger from occurring again.





Contract Rollovers
Futures contracts expire on specific dates and must be rolled over to the next active contract. If you are trading front-month contracts (e.g., ESH5), you need to switch to the new front-month contract before the current one expires.

To manage contract rollovers in WealthCharts:

Identifying expiring contracts - Check the CME contract calendar for your instrument to know when the current contract expires. Volume will shift to the new front-month contract several days before expiration.

Switching contracts - Update the symbol in your charts and DOM to the new front-month contract (e.g., from ESH5 to ESM5)

Using continuous contracts - If you trade using continuous contracts (e.g., ES1), the rollover happens automatically and no action is needed





Risk Management Best Practices
Always use stop-loss orders - Protect every position with a stop to limit downside risk

Use bracket orders - Automate your exits by setting take-profit and stop-loss levels on every trade

Monitor the Liquidation Indicator - Keep an eye on your drawdown distance to avoid unexpected account breaches

Understand Tradeify rules - Your account is subject to Daily Loss Limit (DLL) and Max Trailing Drawdown rules. Hitting either limit will restrict trading or breach your account.

Set User Risk parameters - Configure personal risk limits as an additional safety layer on top of Tradeify's account-level rules

WealthCharts Analytics, Tools & Mobile
Complete guide to WealthCharts analytics with WealthTracker performance tracking, alerts, paper trading, Trading Simulator, and mobile app features for iOS and Android.

February 22, 2026
This guide covers WealthCharts analytics with WealthTracker performance tracking, price alerts, paper trading, the Trading Simulator, and mobile app features for trading on iOS and Android.





WealthTracker


Performance Dashboard
WealthTracker is the built-in analytics tool for tracking your trading performance. Access it from the Main Menu. The Performance Dashboard provides:

P&L tracking - Daily, weekly, and overall profit/loss charts

Win/loss ratios - Percentage of winning vs losing trades

Trade duration analysis - Average hold time for winning and losing trades

Symbol profitability - Performance breakdown by instrument traded

Equity curve - Visual representation of your account growth over time



Trade Journal
WealthTracker automatically records all your trades, creating a detailed trade journal. Review past trades to identify patterns, analyze what worked and what did not, and improve your strategy over time.



Using WealthTracker for Evaluations
WealthTracker is particularly useful when trading Tradeify Evaluation accounts. Use it to:

Track your consistency across trading days

Monitor your progress toward evaluation profit targets

Identify which instruments and setups contribute most to your results

Review your risk management by analyzing losing trades





Alerts


Price Alerts
Set alerts to be notified when an instrument reaches a specific price level. You can create alerts in two ways:

Right-click on chart - Right-click at any price level and select "Set Alert" from the context menu

Price axis - Click on the price axis at the desired level to create an alert



Notification Center
Manage all your alerts from the Notification Center. Alerts can be delivered via:

In-app notifications (popup within WealthCharts)

Email notifications

SMS text message notifications





Paper Trading
WealthCharts includes a built-in paper trading mode that lets you practice trading without risking any capital. Paper trading uses real-time market data but simulated order execution.

Paper trading features include:

Real-time market data with simulated fills

Full DOM and chart trading functionality

Performance tracking with wins, losses, best trade, and worst trade statistics

No financial risk - ideal for testing new strategies or learning the platform





Trading Simulator
The Trading Simulator combines Chart Replay with simulated order execution, allowing you to practice trading on historical data as if it were happening live. Features include:

Custom scenarios - Select any date and instrument to replay

Random scenarios - Let the simulator choose a random historical period for unbiased practice

Speed controls - Adjust playback speed from slow-motion to fast-forward

Hotkeys - Use keyboard shortcuts for quick order entry during replay

Performance tracking - Results are tracked separately from live trading





Mobile Trading


Getting Started
The WealthCharts mobile app is available for iOS and Android:

Download from the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android)

Log in with the same credentials you use on the web platform

Your accounts and settings sync automatically between web and mobile



Mobile DOM
The mobile app includes a full Trading DOM optimized for touchscreens. You can place market, limit, and stop orders, manage bracket orders, and flatten positions directly from your mobile device.



Mobile Charting
Mobile charting includes most features from the web platform:

Multiple chart types and bar styles

Technical indicators

Multiple timeframes

Pinch-to-zoom and scroll navigation



Mobile Portfolio
Monitor your accounts on the go with the mobile Broker Portfolio. View open positions, working orders, account balances, and P&L from anywhere. The mobile portfolio syncs with the web platform in real time.
