Supported Platforms
Tradeify brokers: Tradovate (NinjaTrader, TradingView), Rithmic (Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, R|Trader) or WealthCharts. Same price, chosen at checkout. Switching brokers needs a new account.

Updated over 3 weeks ago
Tradeify supports three broker connections: Tradovate, Rithmic, and WealthCharts. You select your broker at checkout. Each broker has its own credentials and gives you access to different trading platforms.



All brokers are the same price — there is no surcharge for any platform.





Tradovate-Based Platforms
If you select Tradovate as your broker, you receive Tradovate credentials that give you access to the following platforms:



Tradovate — Cloud-based web and desktop platform. Log in at trader.tradovate.com. You must sign the Non-Professional Data Agreement on first login for free market data.

NinjaTrader — Desktop platform that connects using your Tradovate credentials. You must first log in to Tradovate and sign the Non-Professional Data Agreement, or your market data will be delayed.

TradingView — Web-based charting platform that connects via Tradovate. Follow this guide to link your Tradovate account.

Market data: Level 1 CME data is included. Level 2 depth data and EUREX data can be added as paid subscriptions purchased directly from Tradovate — see Level 2 (L2) and EUREX Data: Availability by Broker.







Rithmic-Based Platforms
If you select Rithmic as your broker, you receive Rithmic credentials that give you access to the following platforms:



Tradesea — Next-generation TradingView-based platform with AI features and smart lockout controls.

Quantower — Advanced multi-asset trading and analytics platform.

Sierra Chart — Professional-grade charting and trading platform (requires a paid Sierra Chart subscription). Follow the Sierra Chart Setup Guide to connect.

R|Trader — Rithmic's native trading platform.



All Rithmic-based platforms use the same Rithmic credentials. You can use any of these platforms interchangeably without additional sign-ups.



Market data: Level 1 CME data is included. Level 2 depth data and EUREX data are not yet available on Rithmic accounts and cannot be purchased — coming soon.







WealthCharts
If you select WealthCharts as your broker, you receive WealthCharts credentials that can only be used with the WealthCharts platform. WealthCharts is operated by a third party and uses its own login — WealthCharts access is only included when you select WealthCharts at checkout, not with Tradovate or Rithmic accounts.



Market data: Level 1 CME data is included. Level 2 depth data and EUREX data are not yet available on WealthCharts accounts and cannot be purchased — coming soon.





For setup instructions, see the WealthCharts Platform Overview.





Frequently Asked Questions


Can I switch platforms after purchasing?
You can use any platform compatible with your broker (e.g., if you have Rithmic credentials, you can switch freely between Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, and R|Trader). However, you cannot switch between brokers (e.g., from Tradovate to Rithmic) without purchasing a new account.



Do all platforms cost the same?
Yes. Tradovate, Rithmic, and WealthCharts accounts are all the same price. There is no surcharge for any platform.



Can I use TradingView with a Rithmic account?
No. TradingView connects through Tradovate only. If you want to use TradingView, you need a Tradovate-based account. Rithmic users can use Tradesea as an alternative web-based charting platform.



Can I use NinjaTrader with a Rithmic account?
No. NinjaTrader connects through Tradovate only. If you want to use NinjaTrader, you need a Tradovate-based account.





Are Level 2 and EUREX data available on all brokers?
No. Level 2 (L2) depth of market data and EUREX data are available on Tradovate accounts only, purchased directly from Tradovate. On Rithmic accounts (Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, R|Trader) and WealthCharts accounts these add-ons are not yet available and cannot be purchased — support is coming soon. All accounts include free Level 1 CME data. See Level 2 (L2) and EUREX Data: Availability by Broker.



Which platform should I choose?
Tradovate, Rithmic, and WealthCharts are equal options at the same price. Choose the broker that gives you the platform you want to use. Tradovate is cloud-based, works on any device, and gives you access to NinjaTrader and TradingView. Rithmic gives you access to Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, and R|Trader. WealthCharts gives you access to the WealthCharts platform only.

NinjaTrader 8 Setup Guide
Install and configure NinjaTrader 8 for Tradeify. Covers Tradovate credentials, live data setup, and what to do when no Tradeify or Tradovate connection appears in the NinjaTrader connection list.

July 31, 2026
NOTE: Users are only able to trade their Tradeify accounts under Tradeify's company license with NinjaTrader. Users are NOT able to use their personal license (or any other) on Tradeify accounts.

Before starting, make sure you have logged in to https://trader.tradovate.com/welcome with the user credentials provided on your Tradeify User Dashboard and signed their Non-Professional Data Agreement.

If you do not complete this step, you will not have Live Market Data.

Note that after you complete the above, you may need to wait up to 15 minutes before logging into NinjaTrader to receive your Live Market Data and approval.

Download NinjaTrader desktop by logging in to NinjaTrader with your Tradovate credentials: https://account.ninjatrader.com/welcome


Use the Tradovate credentials from your dashboard to log into NinjaTrader Desktop.


Always choose 'Simulation' if you are on an Evaluation or Sim Funded (aka Lightning Funded) account!






Tradeify or Tradovate Not Showing in the NinjaTrader Connection List
There is no "Tradeify" connection and no "Tradovate" connection in NinjaTrader's connection list. Do not look for one — it does not exist. Tradeify accounts connect using NinjaTrader's own NinjaTrader connection type, with your Tradovate credentials entered into it.



In most cases you never need that list at all: logging into NinjaTrader Desktop with the Tradovate credentials from your Tradeify dashboard at the startup login screen connects you automatically.



If You Use Multi-Provider Mode
If you have Multi-Provider Mode enabled, NinjaTrader Desktop does not show a login screen when it starts, so you must create the connection yourself:

Open the NinjaTrader Desktop Control Center and go to Connections > configure.


In the Available list on the left, double-click NinjaTrader. This is the correct connection type for Tradeify accounts — not NinjaTrader Continuum, not NinjaTrader FXCM, and not Rithmic for NinjaTrader Brokerage.


A connection called My NinjaTrader appears under Configured. You can rename it to Tradeify (or anything else) in the Connection name field.

Enter the Tradovate User name and Password from the top of your Tradeify User Dashboard.

Set Account type to Simulation if you are on an Evaluation or Sim Funded (aka Lightning Funded) account. Never select Live.

Click Apply, then OK. Your new connection is now available from the Connections menu in the Control Center.



All trades made on NinjaTrader Desktop are synced with your Tradovate platform(s) and vice versa.





Manual Lockout
You can voluntarily lock yourself out of trading for a set period using Manual Lockout. This feature is available through Tradovate Web Trader and NinjaTrader Mobile — once activated, the lockout is enforced across all platforms including NinjaTrader 8 desktop.



For full details on duration options and how it works, see Tradovate Risk & Account Settings.

Order Rejections
Symptoms: "Order rejected" messages when trying to place a trade



Common causes:

Restricted instrument - You may be trying to trade a product that isn't supported. Tradeify only supports specific futures contracts. See Supported Trading Products for the full list. Single Stock Futures (SSF) are carried by Tradovate only, so an SSF symbol will be rejected on Rithmic or WealthCharts.

Drawdown breach - If you've hit your trailing drawdown threshold, your account is in liquidation-only mode and cannot open new positions.

Daily Loss Limit (DLL) - If you've reached your daily loss limit, trading is restricted until the next trading day.

Position size limit - You may be exceeding your account's maximum position size.

Custom risk settings - Your personal risk settings in Tradovate may be blocking the order.
